# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Inferred for this session: static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, chosen for a fast, portable portfolio prototype with no backend requirements.

## Users

Inferred from the brief: prospective branding and web-design clients evaluating Sanjid R's taste, capabilities, and fit for a project.

## Product Purpose

Present Sanjid R as an independent designer, showcase selected branding and website work, explain the working approach, and make it easy for a future client to start a conversation.

## Positioning

Sanjid combines brand identity and digital design in one practice, shaping both how an idea is recognized and how it behaves on screen.

## Operating Context

The site is primarily evaluated visually and quickly, often from a link shared directly with a prospective client. The landing page must therefore communicate authorship, range, and a clear contact path without relying on invented proof.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One complete landing-page experience in each of three distinct design directions.
- A comparison page must let the user switch between all three versions.
- Responsive behavior is required for desktop and mobile web.
- No backend, account system, CMS, or persistent data is required for this prototype.
- Project names and descriptions in the prototype are editorial demonstration content and must not be presented as verified client claims.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Sanjid R.
- Services: brand identity, art direction, and website design.
- The supplied references establish a preference for assertive editorial typography, black-led contrast, large imagery, asymmetrical layouts, and restrained navigation.
- The finished work must remain distinct from the references and must not reproduce their brand, copy, or exact compositions.

## Evidence on Hand

- Twelve visual-reference screenshots supplied by the user.
- No verified client list, awards, testimonials, performance statistics, biography details, portrait, or contact address were supplied; the site must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Let selected work lead before biography or process.
- Make brand and web capability feel like one joined practice.
- Prefer memorable composition over generic portfolio grids.
- Keep every claim honest and every call to action usable.
- Preserve legibility and navigation across viewport sizes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic structure, keyboard-visible interactions, reduced-motion support, readable text sizing, and sufficient contrast.
